Privacy Policy

Pixelera Technologies LLP · Last updated November 8, 2026

What Orbathon collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. We’ve kept it short on purpose.

The short version

  • We collect very little: your sign-in details, player name, scores and the shop items you’ve tried.
  • Your player name and best score are public on the leaderboard.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob, which may use your device’s advertising ID.
  • No location, contacts, camera or recording, and we don’t sell your data.
  • You can delete your account any time in Settings.

This summary is only a guide. The full policy below is what counts.

1Who we are

Orbathon is a mobile game made by Pixelera Technologies LLP, based in India (“we”, “us”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the game (the “Game”) collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. It goes together with our Terms of Service.

By tapping Accept in the Game, or by playing it, you agree to this policy. If you don’t agree, please don’t play.

2What we collect

We keep this small. Here is everything the Game collects or stores:

  • Sign-in details. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your account ID and, depending on what you allow, your name and email address (Apple may give us a private relay address instead). We use these to create and recognise your account.
  • Guest ID. If you play as a guest, the Game creates an anonymous account with a random ID, so your score can reach the leaderboard. It holds no name or email.
  • Player name. The name you choose, up to 16 characters.
  • Scores. Your best score and best run time, and the details of a run (its score and length) when it is sent to us, so we can check it is genuine.
  • Shop trials. Which shop items a signed-in account has already tried, so a trial can’t be repeated by reinstalling.
  • Technical logs. Our servers keep ordinary logs, such as your IP address, the time of a request and your account ID, to keep the Game secure and fair.
  • Saved on your device. Your coins, unlocked bubbles, pointers and backgrounds, settings, streak and progress are stored on your own device. We don’t receive them, except for your scores.

3What we don’t collect

Orbathon doesn’t ask for your location, contacts, photos or camera, and it doesn’t record audio. We don’t collect your card or bank details: purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We don’t sell your personal information.

4How we use your information

  • To run the Game: your account, your scores, the leaderboard and shop trials.
  • To keep the leaderboard fair, for example by rejecting scores that look impossible and removing cheats.
  • To show ads and to process purchases, through the services in sections 6 and 7.
  • To answer your messages, fix problems and keep the Game secure.
  • To follow the law and to protect our rights.

5The public leaderboard

Your player name and best score are public. Anyone playing Orbathon can see them. Please don’t put personal information in your name. You can leave the leaderboard by deleting your account (section 10), and we may remove names or scores that break our Terms.

6Ads

Orbathon is free and shows ads, some of which you choose to watch for a reward. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with an ad. We don’t receive this information about you. Google’s own advertising policies explain how it is used.

You can limit ad personalisation in your device settings: on Android under Settings → Google → Ads, and on iPhone under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and Apple Advertising.

7Purchases

Coin packs are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. They process your payment and keep their own records. We never see your card or bank details. The Game only receives confirmation that a purchase was completed, so it can add the coins to your balance.

8Who we share it with

We share information only as needed to run the Game:

  • Everyone playing sees your player name and best score on the leaderboard.
  • Service providers that work for us or alongside the Game: Google and Apple (sign-in, app stores and, for Google, ads) and the online service that stores our account and leaderboard data. They may use the information only to provide their service, under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Authorities or others where the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of players or of us.
  • A buyer or successor if our business is sold or merged, with this policy still applying.

9Where it is kept, and for how long

Account and leaderboard data is stored with our online service provider and may be processed in countries other than your own. We keep it for as long as your account exists, or until we remove inactive guest data. Server logs are kept only for a limited time. Data saved on your device stays there until you delete the Game or clear its data.

10Deleting your data

  • Signed-in players: open Settings → Delete account in the Game. This permanently removes your account, leaderboard score and shop trials from our servers.
  • Guests, or if you can’t open the Game: email us at pixeleratech@gmail.com from the address linked to your account (or, for a guest, tell us your player name) with the subject “Delete my data”, and we will act on it within 30 days.
  • On your device: coins, unlocks and settings are removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Deleting your account does not refund coins or items.

11Your choices and rights

Under the laws that apply to you, which in India include the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to ask what we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to withdraw your consent. To use any of these rights, email pixeleratech@gmail.com. We may need to confirm that the account is yours first.

12Children

Orbathon is for players aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, please use it only with the permission of a parent or guardian. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

13Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including secure connections and access rules on our servers. No method of storing or sending data is completely safe, so we can’t promise absolute security.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add features or when the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. When a change matters, the Game will ask you to accept the new version.

15Contact and grievances

For questions, requests or complaints about your privacy, email pixeleratech@gmail.com. This is also our grievance contact: we aim to acknowledge messages promptly and to reply as soon as we can. To report a child safety concern, email us straight away.
