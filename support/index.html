Report content or a user

Every chatter, comment and profile can be reported inside the app. Tap the ⋯ options and choose Report. Reported content may be hidden while our team reviews it, and we remove content that breaks our rules. You can also Block a user from their profile. If you can't use the app, email us with the details.

To report child safety concerns, email pixeleratech@gmail.com straight away.

Delete your account

In the app
Open Settings, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Without the app
Email pixeleratech@gmail.com from the address linked to your account with the subject “Account Deletion Request”. We process requests within 30 days.

Frequently asked questions

What is a chatter?

A post that feels like a message. Share it with everyone, your friends, a hangout, an event or a community. It's as easy as chatting, but it stays out there for people to see. You can style it with colors, images and special themes.

What is an Instant?

A raw, unedited moment from your day. Take a photo or video with a swipe, tap and post. Nothing gets edited, so what you share is what you saw.

What is Resonance?

A way to respond to a chatter without a crowded comment section. Every resonance stands on its own, so there are no endless reply chains and no pile-ons. Each resonance is seen only by you, and you can choose to reply to the ones you get.

What is Discover?

A calm, simple feed to find what's happening around you. See what people are talking about with #trending, find people who share your interests in hangouts, and find events and communities nearby.

What is a hangout?

A temporary space for people who share an interest.

What is an event?

A real-life get-together. Create an event with a time and place, send invites in seconds, and share updates and photos while it happens.

What is a community?

A home for your people. Communities are one of the places you can host and share a chatter.

Do events disappear?

Yes. When an event is over, it disappears. There's no clutter, just the memories.
