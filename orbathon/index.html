A bubble-and-lasers arcade game.

1About the game

Steer your glowing bubble through a field of lasers, collect coins and chase a higher score on every run. Orbathon is quick to pick up, hard to put down, and free to play on your phone.

2What you get

Public leaderboard

Your player name and best score show up for everyone playing Orbathon.

Daily challenge

A fresh challenge to come back to, so there is always something new to beat.

Shop

Spend your coins on bubbles, pointers and background themes to make the game your own.

Play as a guest or sign in

Jump straight in, or sign in with Google or Apple to link your score to an account.

3Free to play

Orbathon is supported by ads and optional in-app purchases. Some ads are rewarded ads that you choose to watch, for example to double the coins you earned. Coin packs are optional and never required to play.
