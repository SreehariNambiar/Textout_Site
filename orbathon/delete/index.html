Delete Your Account

Pixelera Technologies LLP · Last updated November 13, 2026

How to ask us to delete your Orbathon account and the data linked to it.

1How to request deletion

Option A: in the game (fastest).

  1. Open Orbathon and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and its data are permanently removed from our servers.

Option B: by email, if you are a guest or can’t open the game.

  1. Email pixeleratech@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”.
  2. Send it from the email address linked to your Google or Apple sign-in. If you play as a guest, include your player name instead.
  3. We will act on your request within 30 days and confirm by email.

2What we delete

  • Your account and sign-in details (Google or Apple account ID, and the name and email address we received).
  • Guest accounts: the anonymous ID and everything linked to it.
  • Your player name.
  • Your leaderboard entry: best score, best run time and the run details sent to us.
  • The record of which shop items you have tried.

3What is kept, and for how long

  • Server logs (such as IP address, request time and account ID) used for security and fair play are kept only for a limited time and then removed. They are not used for anything else.
  • Purchase records are held by Apple or Google, who process payments, under their own policies. We never receive your card or bank details.
  • Data we must keep by law, if any, is kept only for the period the law requires.

4Good to know

  • Deletion is permanent and can’t be undone. Coins and unlocked items are not refunded.
  • Coins, unlocks and settings saved on your own device are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • You can also delete only some of your data. Email us and tell us what you would like removed.

For everything we collect and why, read our Privacy Policy.
